How to play

Orbit around the city block and study the clearances. The orange building is condemned; blue-grey buildings are protected.

  • Engineering View: reveal columns, beams, slabs and the central core.
  • Place charges: click glowing structural joints. Charges sever connections rather than throwing the building outward.
  • Shape the fall: remove enough support to trigger progressive collapse. Falling floors now carry impact energy into floors below, so a high-level failure can pancake downward through the structure.
  • Sequence: later contracts reward delayed charge groups to fold tall towers inward.
  • Score: fewer charges, less explosive power and zero collateral score highest.

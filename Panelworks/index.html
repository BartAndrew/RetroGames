HOW TO PLAY PANELWORKS

You're the workshop manager. Your objective is to deliver profitable, high-quality repairs without wrecking insurer confidence.

  1. Choose an inbound insurer referral and click ACCEPT or DECLINE.
  2. After estimation, order the parts required for the repair. Express orders arrive sooner but cost extra.
  3. Assign the appropriate trade at each production stage. Foreman Assist dispatches available staff automatically.
  4. Once repaired, vehicles proceed through paint, assembly, detail and QC. Contact customers when ready.
  5. Deliver the car and get paid. Keep wages, parts and overhead under control.

The 3D workshop can be orbited by dragging and zoomed with the mouse wheel or the +/− buttons. Click a vehicle or a job to inspect its details. Progress is saved to this browser.

# Lefty Liberal asset pack

This folder adds the expanded **Lefty Liberal** animation artwork created from the established Stereotype Fighter character. The full master sheet is preserved, and the movements are also grouped into smaller sheets so they are easier to work with in the game.

## Included sheets

- `lefty-liberal-additional-movements-master.png` — complete expanded movement master.
- `lefty-liberal-ground-mobility-sheet.png` — idle/bob, forward walk, back walk, dash/run, crouch and crouch-walk.
- `lefty-liberal-air-mobility-sheet.png` — neutral jump, forward/back jump, landing and turn-around.
- `lefty-liberal-offense-combos-sheet.png` — punch chain, kick chain and projectile special.
- `lefty-liberal-defense-reactions-sheet.png` — blocking, light/heavy hit reactions and get-up.
- `lefty-liberal-knockdown-taunt-win-lose-sheet.png` — knockdown plus personality/outcome poses.
- `lefty-liberal-original-combat-reference.png` — the earlier combat reference used to keep the character locked.
- `lefty-liberal-animation-map.json` — exact panel coordinates and frame counts for slicing the master sheet in code.

## Character lock

Future poses should preserve the same brown beanie, dark glasses, beard, olive jacket, black **EAT THE RICH** shirt, cuffed dark jeans and black-and-white sneakers. Keep the same proportions, pixel density, outline weight and palette.

## Next pose pass

The next genuinely new drawings should be: low sweep, uppercut/launcher, crouching punch, crouching kick, air punch, air kick, throw/grab, throw-victim reaction, parry, dodge/backstep, dizzy/stun, KO fall, intro pose and character-select portrait.

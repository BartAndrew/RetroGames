# Stereotype Fighters V7.2 — Gym Bro playable rig edition

This is a **playable rig release**, not a claim that all realistic animation sprite sheets and cinematic scenes are final.

## Included
- Rebuilt 20-fighter, 5×4 character selection with a large identity preview, keyboard navigation, controller menu navigation, and touch confirmation.
- Gym Bro Tyler uses an identity-consistent yellow GYM BRO tank, backwards black cap, black shorts, white socks and trainers across 31 canonical rig animation states.
- Separate procedural strike keyposes for Pump Jab, Bicep Cross, Quad Axe, Deadlift Uppercut, low kick, sweep, aerial attacks, Protein Shaker Blast, recovery, intro, victory and Leg Day Tsunami.
- Fixed 60 Hz combat, five super hit offsets, hitboxes, damage and canonical four-rival story unchanged.
- Other fighters preserve their existing renderer/asset paths.
- Historical V7 and V7.1 remain available as separate entry points.

## Quality and limitations
CI verified R15 character selection and R16 V7.2 browser smoke and unit tests on the development branch: https://github.com/BartAndrew/RetroGames/actions/runs/44163843259 .

The realistic 120-frame transparent Gym Bro sprite pack and ten fully art-directed story images remain unapproved, and are **not included** in the production release. Offline candidate ZIPs and in-development action sheets are not production-quality assets. A subsequent art-polish release is required for those.
